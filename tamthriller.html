PISAASU

DIRECTOR: MYSSKIN

REVIEW:

Pisaasu is one of the most unique horror films in Tamil cinema because it is less about fear and more about emotion, loneliness, and compassion. Directed by Mysskin, the movie follows a violinist who begins experiencing paranormal events after trying to save a girl from a tragic accident. Instead of presenting the ghost as purely terrifying, the film slowly reveals an emotional connection between the spirit and the protagonist. What makes Pisaasu special is its atmosphere. Mysskin avoids typical loud horror clichés and instead creates tension using silence, dim lighting, sound design, and slow emotional buildup. The cinematography and background score by Arrol Corelli play a huge role in making the film haunting and immersive. Critics praised the movie for giving a fresh emotional angle to ghost stories and for Mysskin’s bold storytelling style.
