VINNAITHAANDI VARUVAAYAA

DIRECTOR: GAUTHAM VASUDEV MENON

REVIEW:

Vinnaithaandi Varuvaayaa directed by Gautham Vasudev Menon is a romantic drama that has become a modern Tamil cinema classic for many viewers. The film follows Karthik (an aspiring filmmaker) and Jessie, a Malayali Christian girl, as they fall in love while dealing with religion, family pressure, and their own emotional contradictions. What makes the film stand out is not a complex plot, but the way the emotions are told—slow, realistic, and deeply personal.
