MY MISTER

DIRECTOR: Kim Won-seok

REVIEW:

My Mister is a deeply emotional K-drama about a struggling middle-aged man and a young woman burdened by poverty and loneliness. Instead of dramatic tragedy, it shows quiet sadness in everyday life—debt, workplace pressure, emotional neglect, and isolation. The story slowly builds a bond between the two leads as they help each other survive emotionally, not romantically in a typical way.

Common criticisms:

Critically, it is praised for its realistic writing, slow-burn storytelling, and powerful performances that feel very natural rather than exaggerated. Director Kim Won-seok is especially appreciated for using silence, long shots, and minimal music to make emotions feel more real. However, some viewers find it too slow or emotionally heavy because it avoids action and focuses on internal struggles. Overall, it is considered one of the saddest but most meaningful K-dramas, leaving a lasting emotional impact rather than simple heartbreak.

Rating: ★★★★★ (4/5)
