96

DIRECTOR: C. Prem Kumar

REVIEW:

96 is a deeply emotional Tamil romantic drama directed by C. Prem Kumar and starring Vijay Sethupathi and Trisha Krishnan. The film beautifully explores nostalgia, lost love, and the pain of missed opportunities through the reunion of two former school lovers after many years. Instead of relying on heavy drama or twists, the movie focuses on subtle emotions, meaningful conversations, and realistic performances, which made audiences connect strongly with the characters. Vijay Sethupathi’s restrained acting and Trisha’s graceful screen presence are major highlights, while Govind Vasantha’s soulful music adds emotional depth, especially in songs like Kaathalae Kaathalae.
