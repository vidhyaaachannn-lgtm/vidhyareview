THE FAULT IN OUR STARS

Author: JOHN GREEN

REVIEW:

The Fault in Our Stars (by John Green) This novel is a deeply emotional modern love story that follows two teenagers, Hazel Grace Lancaster and Augustus Waters, who meet at a cancer support group. Hazel is a quiet and intelligent girl who has been living with cancer for a long time, while Augustus is a charming and confident boy who has also faced cancer but is in remission. Their relationship begins as friendship but slowly grows into a strong emotional bond and love. The story explores how both characters try to live meaningful lives despite knowing their time may be limited. A major part of the novel is their journey to find answers about life, death, and what it means to be remembered. Augustus surprises Hazel with a trip to Amsterdam so she can meet her favorite author, which becomes a turning point in their relationship. Through their experiences, the book shows both the beauty and pain of young love. The strength of the novel lies in its emotional storytelling. It captures how love can give hope even in suffering. The writing is simple but very impactful, making readers connect deeply with the characters. However, the book also has criticisms. Some readers feel the emotional impact is “too calculated,” as if the story is designed mainly to make readers cry. Augustus is sometimes seen as unrealistic, almost like a perfect ideal partner. Some philosophical dialogues also feel too mature for teenagers. Despite this, the novel remains one of the most powerful and widely read love stories of modern times because of its emotional depth and unforgettable ending.

Rating: ★★★★★ (5/5)
