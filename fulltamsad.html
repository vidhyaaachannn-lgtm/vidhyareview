96

DIRECTOR: C. Prem Kumar

REVIEW:

96 is a deeply emotional Tamil romantic drama directed by C. Prem Kumar and starring Vijay Sethupathi and Trisha Krishnan. The film beautifully explores nostalgia, lost love, and the pain of missed opportunities through the reunion of two former school lovers after many years. Instead of relying on heavy drama or twists, the movie focuses on subtle emotions, meaningful conversations, and realistic performances, which made audiences connect strongly with the characters. Vijay Sethupathi’s restrained acting and Trisha’s graceful screen presence are major highlights, while Govind Vasantha’s soulful music adds emotional depth, especially in songs like Kaathalae Kaathalae. Some viewers may find the pacing slow, but for those who enjoy heartfelt and realistic love stories, 96 delivers a touching and memorable experience that stays with you long after the film ends.

Common criticisms:

Not everyone loved it equally. Some viewers felt:
The pacing is very slow.
The plot is thin and depends mostly on mood and nostalgia.
Ram’s inability to move on for decades felt unrealistic or unhealthy.
Janu spending emotional time with Ram despite being married made some audiences uncomfortable.
A few recent discussions compared it with Past Lives, saying Past Lives handled mature reunion romance with more realism and emotional complexity.

Rating: ★★★★★ (5/5)
