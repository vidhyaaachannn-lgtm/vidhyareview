ATOMIC HABITS

Author: JAMES CLEAR

REVIEW:

Atomic Habits is one of the most popular self-help books that focuses on how small daily habits can create big changes in life. Written by James Clear, the book explains that success does not come from big sudden efforts but from consistent small improvements done every day. The main idea is that even a 1% improvement daily can lead to massive results over time, while small bad habits can slowly harm your progress. The book is divided into simple and practical ideas that anyone can follow. It teaches how to build good habits using four laws: make it obvious, make it attractive, make it easy, and make it satisfying. It also shows how to break bad habits by doing the opposite, such as making them difficult and less rewarding. One of the strongest ideas in the book is identity-based habits, where you focus on becoming the type of person you want to be instead of only focusing on goals.
