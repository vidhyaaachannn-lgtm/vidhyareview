THE BOOK THIEF

Author: Markus Zusak

REVIEW:

The Book Thief by Markus Zusak is a very emotional and heartbreaking story set during World War II in Nazi Germany. It follows a young girl named Liesel who is sent to live with foster parents and finds comfort in books during a time filled with fear, loss, and violence. The story is uniquely narrated by Death, which makes it even more powerful and emotional because it shows how life is fragile during war.
