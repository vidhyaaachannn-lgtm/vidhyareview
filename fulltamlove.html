VINNAITHAANDI VARUVAAYAA

DIRECTOR: GAUTHAM VASUDEV MENON

REVIEW:

Vinnaithaandi Varuvaayaa directed by Gautham Vasudev Menon is a romantic drama that has become a modern Tamil cinema classic for many viewers. The film follows Karthik (an aspiring filmmaker) and Jessie, a Malayali Christian girl, as they fall in love while dealing with religion, family pressure, and their own emotional contradictions. What makes the film stand out is not a complex plot, but the way the emotions are told—slow, realistic, and deeply personal.The biggest strength of the film is its emotional storytelling. Gautham Menon focuses on small moments—glances, conversations, silence—and builds a believable love story that feels very relatable for many young viewers. The chemistry between Silambarasan and Trisha is a major highlight, especially in the first half. The music by A.R. Rahman is often considered one of the film’s biggest pillars. Songs like “Hosanna” and “Anbil Avan” elevate the romance and are tightly woven into the narrative rather than feeling like interruptions. Cinematography and visual tone also stand out, giving the film a soft, dreamy quality that matches its romantic mood.

Common criticisms:

The second half is often considered slower and slightly repetitive in emotional beats. Some viewers feel the storytelling becomes stretched and could have been tighter. The ending, while realistic, may not satisfy those expecting a conventional romantic closure.

Rating: ★★★★★ (4/5)
